Our Courses

Courses We Offer

Undergraduate, Diploma, Postgraduation, Certifications, Specialization in AI fields and Industry-Oriented Training. Courses are offered in Science & Technology, Law, Textile, Management, and many other disciplines.

Undergraduate

The university offers undergraduate programs in Engineering, Computer Science, Commerce, Arts, and Science. These programs build strong academic foundations, practical skills, and industry readiness, preparing students for higher studies and successful careers.

Management

Management courses such as BBA, MBA, and Executive Programs develop leadership, strategic thinking, and decision-making abilities through industry projects, case studies, and experiential learning.

Postgraduate

Postgraduate programs include M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc, MA, and other specialized Master's degrees. These programs enhance subject expertise, promote innovation, and encourage research-based learning.

Our Facilities

The university provides modern classrooms, advanced laboratories, a rich library, sports facilities, hostels, medical services, and cafeterias to ensure a complete learning environment.

World Class Library

The library provides thousands of books, journals, digital resources, online databases, internet access, and comfortable reading spaces for academic excellence.

Largest Playground

The university features spacious playgrounds and basketball courts where students can practice, compete in tournaments, and maintain physical fitness.

Tasty & Healthy Food

The cafeteria serves hygienic, nutritious, and affordable meals in a comfortable environment, making it a favorite place for students to relax.
